## 2. German Clock Time (Uhrzeit)

### 2.1 Two Systems: Colloquial vs. Official

| System | Usage | Example for 14:30 |
|--------|-------|--------------------|
| **Colloquial (12h)** | Everyday speech | "halb drei" |
| **Official (24h)** | Radio, trains, formal | "vierzehn Uhr dreißig" |

### 2.2 Colloquial Time Vocabulary

| German | Pronunciation | English | Bangla |
|--------|---------------|---------|--------|
| Es ist … | ess ist | It is … | এখন … |
| Uhr | oor | o'clock | টা |
| nach | nahkh | past / after | পরে / বেশি |
| vor | fohr | to / before | আগে / বাকি |
| Viertel nach | FEER-tel nahkh | quarter past | সোয়া |
| Viertel vor | FEER-tel fohr | quarter to | পৌনে |
| halb | halp | half (to NEXT hour!) | পরের ঘণ্টার আধা আগে |
| Mittag | MIT-tahg | noon | দুপুর |
| Mitternacht | MIT-ter-nakht | midnight | মধ্যরাত |

### 2.3 Key German Time Rule: "halb" = Half TO Next Hour

This is the **most confusing** rule for learners:

| Time | German | Literal | English |
|:----:|--------|---------|---------|
| 2:30 | halb drei | half three | half past two |
| 7:30 | halb acht | half eight | half past seven |
| 12:30 | halb eins | half one | half past twelve |

German `halb drei` = "halfway to three" = **2:30**, NOT 3:30.

Finnish has the same pattern: `puoli neljä` = "half four" = 3:30.

## 5. German Date Format (Datum)

### 5.1 Ordinal Numbers for Dates

| Date | German Ordinal | Rule |
|:----:|----------------|------|
| 1st | der erste | Irregular |
| 2nd | der zweite | +te |
| 3rd | der dritte | Irregular |
| 4th | der vierte | +te |
| 7th | der siebte | Irregular |
| 8th | der achte | +te |
| 20th | der zwanzigste | +ste (from 20) |
| 31st | der einunddreißigste | +ste |

**Rule:** 1–19 add `-te` (with irregulars); 20+ add `-ste`.

### 5.2 Date Phrasing

| German | English | Bangla (Proj.) |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Welches Datum ist heute? | What is today's date? | আজকের তারিখ কত? |
| Heute ist der dritte März. | Today is March 3rd. | আজ তেসরা মার্চ। |
| Am fünfzehnten Juni. | On June 15th. | পনেরোই জুন। |
| Wann hast du Geburtstag? | When is your birthday? | তোমার জন্মদিন কবে? |
| Am zweiten Oktober. | On October 2nd. | দোসরা অক্টোবর। |

### 5.3 Date Format Conventions

| Format | Example | Usage |
|--------|---------|-------|
| DD.MM.YYYY | 03.03.2024 | Standard German written format |
| der + ordinal + month | der dritte März | Spoken format |
| am + ordinal-en + month | am dritten März | "on" a specific date |

## 7. Cross-Language Time Concept Comparison

### 7.1 "Half Past" — The Big Confusion

| Language | "2:30" | Logic |
|----------|--------|-------|
| English | half past two | half AFTER the hour |
| Bangla | আড়াইটা (āṛāitā) | unique word |
| German | halb drei | half TO three (next hour!) |
| Finnish | puoli kolme | half three (same as German) |
| Chinese | 两点半 | two o'clock half (after) |
| Portuguese | duas e meia | two and half (after) |
| Arabic | الثَّانِيَةُ وَالنِّصْفُ | the second and the half (after) |

**German and Finnish** share the "half to next hour" convention — a major stumbling block documented in the Finnish demo's English note: *"Finnish says 'half four'"*.

### 7.2 "Quarter" Conventions

| Language | "3:15" | "3:45" |
|----------|--------|--------|
| English | quarter past three | quarter to four |
| German | Viertel nach drei | Viertel vor vier |
| Finnish | varttia yli kolme | varttia vaille neljä |
| Chinese | 三点一刻 | (use 差 pattern) |
| Portuguese | três e um quarto | quinze para as quatro |
| Arabic | الثَّالِثَةُ وَالرُّبْعُ | السَّادِسَةُ إِلَّا الرُّبْعَ |
