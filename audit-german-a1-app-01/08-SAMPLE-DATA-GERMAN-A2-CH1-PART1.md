## Learning Objectives (14)

1. Tell what happened yesterday, last week, and at the weekend in the Perfekt tense
2. Choose haben or sein correctly with movement, change of state, and ordinary verbs
3. Use time markers: gestern, vorgestern, letzte Woche, am Wochenende, danach, zuerst, später, schließlich
4. Build longer A2 answers with reasons using weil and extra details
5. Ask and answer about experience: Hast du schon einmal...?
6. Retell a short story in connected German with accurate word order
7. Practice irregular participles, separable verbs, and -ieren verbs
8. Use English and Bangla support to understand every grammar point deeply
9. Use at least 40 common Partizip-II forms in real A2 sentences
10. Compare short and long A2 answers for oral exam style responses
11. Use connectors: dann, danach, deshalb, trotzdem, außerdem, am Ende
12. Write and speak a 90-second story about a difficult day
13. Ask follow-up questions in the past tense during a conversation
14. Describe problems, excuses, delays, plans, and solutions in the past
