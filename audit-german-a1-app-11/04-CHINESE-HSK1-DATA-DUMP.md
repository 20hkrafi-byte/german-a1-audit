## 6. Pedagogical Context

The Novice Start section serves as a **zero-barrier entry point** for absolute beginners:

1. **8 essential phrases** covering: greeting, gratitude, apology, reassurance, name exchange, nationality
2. **4-layer comprehension:** Chinese characters → Pinyin romanization → English → Bangla
3. **Individual + sequential audio:** Each sentence has a 🔊 button, plus "Play All" for continuous listening
4. **Example question** demonstrates the MCQ format used in the locked Listening/Reading papers
5. **Strategy tiles** preview the solving approach for each exam section

> [!NOTE]
> The 8 starter sentences are specifically chosen for Bangladeshi learners (sentence 6 uses "Karim" as the name, sentence 8 identifies as "Bangladeshi"). This localization pattern matches the platform's target audience.
